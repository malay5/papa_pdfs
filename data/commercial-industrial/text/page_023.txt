Price your own custom structural shapes:
1. Select the strip width of material to be used
2. Prices in chart below are for 100 Linear Feet
Multiply the length of the part times the price per Linear Feet and divide by 100
This will provide the material price for the part length
3. Add $2.10 for each break
4. Pricing includes banding material for shipment
Note:
. Maximum closed angle is 45 degrees
. Hems cannot be fabricated
. Slight metal fracturing may occur at bend lines
. Minimum break dimension is 1%"
. All shapes are subject to tolerances published by MBMA
