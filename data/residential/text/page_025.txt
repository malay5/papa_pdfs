Price your own special trim:
1. Calculate the number of square feet of material (girth x length).
2. Multiply number of square feet by the appropriate square foot price (see chart below).
3. Add $1.60 for each break and $3.20 for each hem.
4. Add appropriate packaging costs.
5. Specify the side with the color or finished coat.
6. Send sketch of the special flashing to with order.
7. If more than one special trim is required, number them SF1, SF2, SF3, etc.
